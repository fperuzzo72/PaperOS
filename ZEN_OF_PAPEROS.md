# The Zen of PaperOS

> *Inspired by the spirit of simple, durable and calm computing.*

The purpose of PaperOS is not to demand attention.

The purpose of PaperOS is to quietly accompany thought.

---

- Thought before technology.
- Documents before applications.
- Calm before urgency.
- Simplicity before features.
- Understanding before automation.
- Longevity before novelty.
- Open formats before proprietary convenience.
- The keyboard is an instrument of thought.
- Hardware is temporary. Ideas endure.
- Learn from history without becoming trapped by it.
- Constraints reveal what truly matters.
- Interfaces should disappear behind the work.
- The filesystem is part of the user experience.
- Compatibility preserves knowledge.
- Stability builds trust.
- Every feature must justify its existence.
- The user owns the data.
- Curiosity should always be rewarded.
- A platform is greater than any device.
- Software should respect human attention.

---

## The PaperOS Question

Before writing code, designing an interface or proposing a new feature, ask:

> **Does this help people think?**

If the answer is yes, continue.

If the answer is no, reconsider.

---

*"The highest purpose of a computer is not to become the center of attention.*

*Its highest purpose is to disappear."*
