# Preface Notes

## Working Opening

> Every generation reimagines the computer.
>
> Few stop to ask what should never have changed.

This book explores that question.

It is not a rejection of progress.

It is an invitation to recover ideas that have already proven their value and carry them into the future.

## Foundational Statement

> **PaperOS is not an attempt to return to the past.
> It is an attempt to carry its best ideas into the future.**

This sentence should guide the tone of the entire book. It makes clear that
PaperOS is not driven by nostalgia, but by careful observation of enduring
ideas that deserve to survive technological change.
